The [roadmap](../roadmap/) has new items, most of them from things I ran into while practising. Nothing here is built yet, and there are no dates; the order is what I intend, not a promise.

## Next

- **Play for N passes** (#111 for rudiments, #113 for a song segment). Today the *loop* switch repeats until you stop it. It becomes a count: off, 1, 2, and so on up to unlimited, and the exercise stops by itself after the last pass. It works for Listen and for recording; when you record N passes, each pass is graded as its own take, with one summary line at the end so you can see which pass was best. The same control will sit on the Score and Rudiments tabs, and the controller can step it.
- **Keep the screen awake** (#115). Some systems lock the screen while you play, because you are not touching the keyboard or the mouse. The program will keep the display awake while it is open, with a switch in **Settings** to turn that off. Windows comes first, since that is the system I can try.

## Later

- **Enduro mode** (#112). Routines made of rudiments, each with its own tempo and length in minutes, saved as files you can edit by hand and share, plus an editor in the program. You will be able to play a routine as a warm-up along with the click, or record it and get graded step by step. This replaces the older "practice sets" idea, which I closed as a duplicate.
- **Options on every panel** (#114). Click, count-in, volume and the rest of the metronome and listening options move onto the panel you are using, so you do not have to go back to **Settings** to change them. Settings keeps what is set once per machine: the sound output, the MIDI ports and the note map.
- **MIDI clock while recording** (#110). Listen and the Metronome tab already send it; recording waits for a bigger change to how timing works inside the program.

> None of this is promised, and the order can change. If something here matters to you, or you would rather have something else first, say so with the *Idea* form linked at the bottom of the [roadmap](../roadmap/).
