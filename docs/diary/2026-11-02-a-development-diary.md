Roll Model has a manual for how to use it and a changelog for what each version changed. Neither says what is going on between releases, or what is coming. This diary does.

## What you will find here

- **Entries** like this one: what changed, why it matters to someone who practises, and what is not tried yet. They are short, and written for drummers.
- **The [roadmap](../roadmap/)**: *Now*, *Next* and *Later*. It is made from the issues of the project, so it is as current as the issue list. There are no dates: the program is made by one person, with a lot of help from an AI, and a date would be a promise I cannot keep.
- **A feed**, for a feed reader: `feed.xml` in this folder.

## Have an idea?

Suggest it as an issue with the *Idea* form (the link is at the bottom of the roadmap). I read them all. An idea that is taken up moves onto the roadmap and then into an entry here.

> Roll Model was made with extensive use of AI, directed and reviewed by its author. Check anything that matters, and tell me when something is wrong.
