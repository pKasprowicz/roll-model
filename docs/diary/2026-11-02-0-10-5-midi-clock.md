Version 0.10.5 can send **MIDI clock** to the MIDI output. A device that follows an external clock (a drum module's own metronome or pattern player, a looper, a DAW) then runs at the tempo of the score, and starts and stops with it.

## What it does

- **Listen** and the **Metronome** tab send 24 clocks to every quarter note, a *Start* before the music and a *Stop* when it ends or you stop it.
- It follows the tempo as it is played: tempo changes in the score, the practice tempo, the ramp and the metronome's BPM.
- **When Start goes out is your choice**: with the count-in, so the device runs through it, or on the first beat of the music.
- An option, off by default, sends the *position* of the first bar when Listen starts from a later bar, for devices that can jump into the middle of a song.
- It is off until you switch it on, under **Settings → Sound**.

## What it does not do yet

> **Not tried on a device.** The messages and their timing are tested against a fake output, but no drum module has heard them yet. The V31 trial is next (#109), and the choices above (where Start goes, what happens when Listen loops) may change after it.

Recordings (takes) do not send the clock yet: they run on an older part of the program that is being replaced.

## Also in this version

Mostly work you will not see: the recording, the metronome, Listen and the drum module's input are tidied into parts that are tested on their own, which makes the next features safer to add. One visible effect: the message you get when nothing is switched on to be heard is now the same everywhere.

Next up: trying the clock and the MIDI port opening with the V31 itself. The [roadmap](../roadmap/) shows what waits for a device.
